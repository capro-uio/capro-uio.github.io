Athanasia Mo Mowinckel

Researcher

  • Data engineering
  • R
  • Web apps
  • Workshops

Mo is a researcher at LCBC, where she has run data processing and management for the EU Lifebrain consortium, and she leads CAPRO. She writes the R packages the work runs on — ggseg, ggseg3d, nettskjemar — and teaches research methods at the department. If your data is spread across sixty files and you want one documented script that pulls it together, that is her work.
