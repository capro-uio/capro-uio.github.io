Markus has a PhD in psychology and is a chief engineer at LCBC. He sets up the
MRI side of a study — working with the physicists at the hospital to keep
protocols valid and current, and keeping collection running smoothly once it
starts — and then does the fMRI analysis, on machines larger than a laptop
when it needs them. If your imaging study needs a protocol that will still
hold up when you come to analyse it, he is the one to ask.
