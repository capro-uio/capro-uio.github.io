Markus is a chief engineer at LCBC, and his own research uses fMRI and
psychophysics to study how visual memory works in the brain. He sets up the
MRI side of a study — working with the physicists at the hospital to keep
protocols valid and current, and keeping collection running once it starts —
and then does the analysis, on machines larger than a laptop when it needs
them. If your imaging study needs a protocol that will still hold up when you
come to analyse it, he is the one to ask.
