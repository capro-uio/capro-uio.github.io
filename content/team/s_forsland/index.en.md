Silje is a research adviser at LCBC, with a BSc in psychology and an MSc in
health psychology from London. She has worked with data collection for years
and plans the process end to end — recruiting and supervising the assistants
who run it, and keeping quality control in place while it does. She cares that
the process stays tidy and documented, and that the participant has a good
time being tested.
