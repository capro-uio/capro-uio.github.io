José Luis has a BSc in engineering, an MSc in applied physics and a PhD in
evolutionary biology, and uses all three on neuroimaging data. He works on
structural and diffusion MRI, and on the analysis that turns a pile of scans
into something you can write about. If your MRI study needs a pipeline and a
statistical plan, he is the one to ask.
