Inge is a chief engineer at LCBC and has worked on some of the largest
neuroimaging datasets there are — Lifebrain, UK Biobank, the Human Connectome
Project, ABCD. He builds the infrastructure the analysis runs on: containers,
cloud, and the secure setups that keep participant data where it is allowed to
be. If a job keeps failing on TSD and the error message means nothing to you,
he is the one to ask.
