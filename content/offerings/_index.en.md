item:
  - name: GOAT
    icon: "fa-solid fa-database"
    tagline: A research database that keeps its own history
    content: |
      Every value is versioned and timestamped, so you can see what changed,
      when, and who changed it. Built through UiO's HUB-node initiative, and
      installed inside TSD when the data is sensitive. We set it up, shape it
      around your project, and train the people who will use it.

  - name: Nettskjema
    icon: "fa-solid fa-clipboard-list"
    tagline: Surveys, from the form to the dataset
    content: |
      Nettskjema is UiO's survey tool, and we work at both ends of it. We help
      build the questionnaire — question types, branching, validation — and the
      process around it: who gets the link, where responses land, who may see
      them. If you want the responses in R afterwards, we can set up our
      nettskjemar package for you — or show your group how to use it
      themselves.

  - name: MRI
    icon: "fa-solid fa-brain"
    tagline: From protocol to published figure
    content: |
      Protocol development, study design and feasibility up front. Once the
      scans exist — from whichever site collected them — we organise them,
      quality-control them and get them onto TSD, then run the preprocessing,
      structural and functional analysis, statistics, and the figures and
      methods text the paper needs.

  - name: Behavioural experiments
    icon: "fa-solid fa-flask"
    tagline: Tasks that run in any browser
    content: |
      Cognitive paradigms, questionnaires and response-time tasks written in
      JavaScript, so the same experiment runs in the lab, on a participant's
      own machine or on a tablet. Our jsPsych Studio assembles one without you
      writing any, and exports a self-contained experiment you can upload to
      Cognition.run, Pavlovia or JATOS, or host on a server of your own. A UiO
      platform to run them on is in development with the IT department.
